What's new in Kharcha 1.1.0:

- **Review every import.** After importing a PhonePe statement you see every payment it added. Undo the whole import if it was the wrong statement, or pick the payments you don't want and delete them. Settings → Imports undoes an import later.
- **Delete several payments at once.** Press and hold a payment in Spends or Inbox, tap more, then Delete. Deleted payments wait under Spends → Deleted, where you can restore them. Importing the same statement again doesn't bring them back: the import shows them as deleted, with a Restore button.
- **Budgets per category in one place** (Home → Budgets): Food ₹10,000, Travel ₹5,000 and so on. The monthly total is now the sum of your category budgets, unless you set a total of your own.
- **Google Drive backup.** Sync now, an automatic sync every day, and your data back after reinstalling the app. It stays off until you connect it in Settings.

The app is now called **Kharcha** on your home screen, with a new icon.

The app now has internet permission, used only for Google Drive backup.

Installs over 1.0.0 and keeps your data.
